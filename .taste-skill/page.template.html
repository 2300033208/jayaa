What the study found

Clocks do see buried mass. A field gravimeter on the same stations sees it far more strongly, in every scenario the simulator ships with.

690–13 000×Gravimeter SNR over clock SNR, five scenarios
10⁻²¹Clock precision per station before a joint survey helps
9 / 9Published values the engine reproduces
324Automated tests, all passing

Bondarescu and colleagues proposed in 2012 that clocks and gravimeters together would pin down depth. Nobody had tested it. This project does, and with any clock that exists or is planned, adding the clock leaves the depth error unchanged.
